Choose your architecture
Architecture describes the type of processor an app is built to run on. Choose the one used by the computer running Docker.
ARM64
Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later), Raspberry Pi with a 64-bit OS, and other ARM-based computers.
AMD64
Most Intel and AMD desktop PCs, laptops, servers, and Intel-based Macs. Also called x86-64; it is used by Intel as well as AMD.
On a Raspberry Pi, check that the OS is 64-bit. ARM64 images do not work with a 32-bit OS.
Matching your processor gives the best performance. Running another architecture needs Docker emulation, which may be slower or unavailable. Missing images produce an error; your selection is never changed automatically.
